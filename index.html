Product Intelligence

Product Intelligence is a market intelligence research platform for analyzing public ecommerce ranking and market trend data.

Purpose

The application collects and analyzes publicly available ecommerce ranking information, product trends, and historical market signals for product research.

Current Status

The platform is currently under private development and is used for founder product-market research.

Data Principles

The platform prioritizes traceable public data sources, source attribution, historical evidence, and transparent market analysis.
